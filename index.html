What's inside

This easy-to-read, laugh-out-loud guide shows you exactly what to tap and exactly what to type, one simple step at a time.

  • Turn three eggs and a lonely zucchini into dinner
  • Write a birthday toast that makes the whole table cry (the good kind)
  • Catch scammers who fake a grandchild's voice
  • Get ready for doctor visits, plan trips, and fix old photos
  • Keep your private information private

No jargon. No condescension. Just big type, plain English, and a “Try It Now” box and tear-out cheat sheet in every chapter.
